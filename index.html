My values & product philosophy

Four principles that guide my product decisions, from discovery to delivery.

User-centricity

Every product decision starts with a deep understanding of real user needs. Before thinking solution, I seek to understand the problem: interviews, field observation, behavior analysis.

Outcome over output

What matters isn't the number of features shipped, but the measurable impact on users and business. Every decision should connect back to a concrete result.

Data-informed, not data-driven

Data informs my decisions but never replaces product judgment. Metrics give me the "what", user research gives me the "why".

Curiosity & experimentation

I'm naturally curious, especially about AI — I enjoy testing new tools, prototyping fast, and learning by doing rather than waiting for the perfect plan.
